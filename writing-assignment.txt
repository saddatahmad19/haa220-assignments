# Writing Assignment

The object I chose is a seated bronze statue of Buddha, most likely from China's Northern Wei Dynasty. It features a dark, almost black patina, a stylized lotus base, and a large, circular halo carved with intricate patterns. The body rises vertically from the base, while the head, sholders, and arms create a strong sense of balance from left to right. Even though the statue contains many small details, the overall form is simple and controlled. The body, legs, and arms are mostly made up of smooth curves, while the base gives the bottom a solid and straight appearance.

The head carries the decorative detail. The hair is rendered as rows of snail-shell curls that rise over a domed cranial bump, which then tapers into a slender flame with grooved upward-curving tongues. In contrast the face is smooth and simplified. It is an oval with brows, downcast eyelids with crisply incised edges, a straight nose and a faint smile all of which convey a quiet inward focus. The long thin earlobes curve outward. Hang to about jaw level a deliberate elongation rather than a natural proportion.

The waist is brad through the sholders. It tapers to a narrow torso with only two small raised dots marking the nipples. A single robe is draped over the sholder, sweeping diagonally across the chest leaving the right sholder exposed. It's edges are shown by engraved lines and a narrow pleated tail hangs near the belly button. Due to the fabric being so thin and lacking folds, the body reads clearly beneath it and the drapery functions as a linear accent rather than a concealing layer.
