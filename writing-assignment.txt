# Writing Assignment

The object I chose is a seated bronze statue of Buddha, most likely from China's Northern Wei Dynasty. It features a dark, almost black patina, a stylized lotus base, and a large, circular halo carved with intricate patterns. The body rises vertically from the base, while the head, sholders, and arms create a strong sense of balance from left to right. Even though the statue contains many small details, the overall form is simple and controlled. The body, legs, and arms are mostly made up of smooth curves, while the base gives the bottom a solid and straight appearance.
